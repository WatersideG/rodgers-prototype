How long does a fitting take?
Plan on 60–90 minutes for a full fit; race builds run longer. Appointments recommended; required for custom race work.
What should I bring?
Your current boots, footbeds if you have them, and the socks you actually ski in.
Do race boots include service?
No — race boot purchase does not include service. The race-prep menu above covers the work.
