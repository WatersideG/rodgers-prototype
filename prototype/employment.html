Home  /  About  /  Employment

Sales associates and ski technicians at both stores, full-time and part-time, seasonal and year-round. No experience required; we’ll train. A love for outdoor activities is the requirement.

Rodgers hires for the winter season in Lincoln, New Hampshire and Scarborough, Maine: people who ski, ride or bike and want to help others do the same. Openings are posted here and on Indeed; you can also stop in at either store and fill out an application.

Open positions

Sales associate

Lincoln, NH and Scarborough, ME · Full-time or part-time, seasonal

Help skiers, riders and families find the right gear and get them out the door happy. No experience required; we’ll train. A love for outdoor activities is the requirement.

Ski technician

Lincoln, NH and Scarborough, ME · Full-time or part-time, seasonal

Tunes, mounts and race prep on the Montana machines, alongside techs who have been doing this for years. No experience required; we’ll train.

Apply

Openings are a Payload collection (title, store, type, description, active flag), so the shop can post and close a role without a developer.

Why work here

Same experts, bigger vision

A family-run shop since 1974, a Ski Magazine Gold Medal Shop, and the first stop for skiers coming off I-93. You will learn boot fitting from Masterfit and Sidas certified fitters, tuning on Montana machines, and the race business from techs who prep FIS skis. Rodgers Ski & Sport is an equal opportunity employer.
