Junior Seasonal Lease

Home  /  Scarborough, ME  /  Junior Seasonal Lease

Skis, bindings and boots for the whole season at the Scarborough, Maine store. $159. Pick-ups begin October 1, no appointment needed.

$

Cost

$159
for the season. Includes skis, bindings and boots.

1

Pick-up

Lease pick-ups begin October 1. No appointment needed; walk in and one of our staff will get you outfitted.

2

Return

Latest drop-off is May 1, unless you are still skiing (call to let us know and avoid late fees). Drop-offs after the month of May are subject to a $159 charge.

Who it fits

Kids grow. Leases fit.

For kids age 2 to 13, up to 130 lbs. Beginner to intermediate. Ski sizes 70 to 150 cm; boot sizes 14.5 to 26.5.

The lease is a Scarborough program. The Lincoln, NH store sells junior packages and rents by the day instead.

Inquire by email Call (207) 883-3669

Lease questions

Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk in from October 1 and one of our staff will get your skier outfitted.
What if we’re still skiing in May?
Call and let us know so you avoid late fees. Drop-offs after the month of May are subject to a $159 charge.
Can I reserve the lease online?
Not this season. Online lease sign-up is planned for next year; for now, email or call the Scarborough store.
