The tune room

Montana stone grinder, race-room standards

Drop-off questions

How long does a tune take?
It depends on the season. We state current turnaround at drop-off. Holiday weeks run longer, and day-of race tunes need an appointment.
Do you tune snowboards?
Yes. Board tunes run through the same tune room. Ask at the desk for the board rate.
Do you service bikes?
Yes, all summer. Bronze, Silver and Gold bike tunes are on the Bikes page.
