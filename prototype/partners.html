Mountains and organizations in the Lincoln area.

Resort community · Lincoln, NH

South Peak Resort

A 360-acre slopeside community on Loon Mountain in Lincoln, with homesites, townhomes, condos and rentals. Rodgers is minutes from the resort on Railroad Street.

Website
Ski area · Lincoln, NH

Loon Mountain Resort

Year-round adventure in New Hampshire’s White Mountains. The Lincoln store is right down the street: gear up, rent, or tune before you reach the base lodge.

Website
Ski area · Franconia, NH

Cannon Mountain

The state-run ski area in Franconia Notch State Park, a short drive north of Lincoln on I-93.

Website
Ski area · Bretton Woods, NH

Bretton Woods

New Hampshire’s largest ski area, with alpine and Nordic skiing at the Omni Mount Washington Resort.

Website
Chamber · Lincoln & Woodstock, NH

Western White Mountains Chamber of Commerce

Promotes the Lincoln and Woodstock area as a basecamp for the White Mountains: lodging, dining, events and seasonal activities.
